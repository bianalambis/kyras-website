FAQs

Everything you need to know before entering the underground.

GENERAL

Getting Started

K'YRAS is an underground electronic music club and collective established in 2019. We host techno, house, experimental, and ambient music events in a multi-room venue in the city's industrial district. More than a club, we're a community dedicated to authentic underground culture and sonic exploration.
Our venue is located at Sector 7, Unit 42 in the Industrial District. We don't publish the exact address publicly. When you purchase a ticket or RSVP to an event, you'll receive coordinates and directions 24 hours before the event. This helps us maintain the intimate, underground nature of our space.
Hours may vary by event. Our typical operating hours are from 8:00pm to 3:00am Wednesday through Sunday. We are closed Mondays and Tuesdays.
Yes, K'YRAS is strictly 21+ for all events. Valid government-issued photo ID is required at the door. No exceptions. This policy helps us maintain a mature, respectful environment for our community.
ENTRY

Getting In

Tickets are available through our website and select partner platforms. We recommend buying in advance as many events sell out. Members get early access and priority entry. Walk-ins are possible for some events but capacity is limited and we cannot guarantee entry.
We don't have a formal dress code, but we encourage self-expression and effort. All-black is always appropriate. Creative, dark, industrial, or futuristic aesthetics are celebrated. We discourage mainstream club attire (button-downs, dress shoes) and sportswear. When in doubt: if you'd wear it to a rave, you're probably good.
Yes. Even with a ticket, entry is at door staff discretion. We turn away people who appear intoxicated, aggressive, or don't align with our community values. This isn't about exclusivity — it's about maintaining a safe, respectful environment. Come with good energy and you'll be welcome.
Members have automatic guestlist access with priority entry. For non-members, guestlist is available for select events and through our Friday residency (free before midnight). Sign up through our mailing list or Telegram for guestlist announcements.
Membership is by application only. We review applications monthly and prioritize those who have attended multiple events and demonstrated respect for our space and community. Benefits include early ticket access, priority entry, exclusive events, and access to members-only areas. Apply through our contact form.
AT THE VENUE

Inside K'YRAS

Photography is only permitted in designated areas (typically the terrace and lounge areas). No photos or videos on the main dancefloors. This policy protects the privacy of our guests and preserves the immersive experience. Phone cameras will be covered with stickers at entry. Repeat violators may be asked to leave.
Yes, we have a staffed coat check available for a small fee. We strongly recommend using it — the dancefloor gets hot and you don't want to carry your jacket all night. We're not responsible for items left unattended in the venue.
We are a cashless venue. All bars and services accept credit/debit cards and mobile payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay). This speeds up service and improves security. ATMs are not available on-site.
Light snacks and refreshments are available at our lounge bar. For longer events, we sometimes have food vendors on the terrace (weather permitting). We recommend eating before you arrive for extended sessions.
The main floor (The Void) and bar areas are wheelchair accessible with a ground-level entrance. The Drift room is accessible via elevator. The Signal terrace has limited accessibility due to the building's structure. Please contact us in advance if you have specific accessibility needs and we'll do our best to accommodate.
SAFETY

Your Wellbeing

Safety is our priority. We have trained security staff throughout the venue, medical personnel on-site for major events, and designated "safe spaces" where you can decompress. Our staff are trained in harm reduction and de-escalation. If you ever feel unsafe, find any staff member — we're here to help.
Zero tolerance. Any form of harassment, discrimination, or unwanted contact will result in immediate removal and a permanent ban. No warnings. No second chances. This applies to everyone — guests, staff, and artists alike. Report any incidents to security or bar staff immediately.
Yes. Free water stations are available throughout the venue. Stay hydrated, especially if you're dancing for extended periods. Our bar staff will also provide water on request at no charge.
Yes, bring earplugs. Our sound system is powerful and we don't compromise on volume. We provide free basic earplugs at the coat check, but we recommend bringing your own high-fidelity earplugs that preserve sound quality while protecting your hearing. Your ears will thank you.
